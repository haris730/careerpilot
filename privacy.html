[BUSINESS NAME] provides CareerPilot. This page explains, in plain language, how information may be handled when you use the service.

CV and account data

If you sign in and save CVs, your account details and saved CV information may be stored in our Supabase database so the service can load your CVs across sessions. The information you enter can include your name, contact details, work history, education, skills and other CV content.

CareerPilot may also keep CV editor data in your browser's local storage so your work can remain available on that browser. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to access locally stored information. Sign out on shared devices and avoid saving sensitive information on a shared browser.

AI features

When you use an AI feature, the text you submit for that feature (such as CV content, a job description or a cover-letter request) is sent to an AI service provider to generate a response. Do not submit information you do not want processed for that purpose. The provider may handle submitted text under its own terms and privacy practices.

Job listings

Job listings are obtained from third-party providers. When you follow an application link, you go to the original provider or employer website, which has its own privacy practices and terms.

Retention and deletion requests

We may ask for reasonable information to verify the request. Some information may remain for a limited time in backups or where retention is necessary for security or legal reasons.
