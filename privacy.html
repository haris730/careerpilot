Services and information

CareerPilot uses Supabase for account sign-in and account data, saved CVs, and AI usage counters. Google sign-in is offered through Supabase's Google authentication provider.

CareerPilot is hosted on Cloudflare. Its Cloudflare Worker uses Workers AI to process text submitted to AI features and generate responses. Text you type or submit in an AI feature is processed by the AI model to produce that feature's result.

Job-alert settings, including the email address associated with your signed-in account, may be stored in Cloudflare D1 so alerts can be managed and checked. Application-tracker entries, saved jobs, and CV editor data are also stored in your browser's local storage. The CV copy kept in local storage may remain on that browser even when you use the service elsewhere.

Information you enter can include your name, contact details, work history, education, skills, job preferences, application details and other CV or AI-request text.

Other people using the same browser profile may be able to access locally stored information. Sign out on shared devices and avoid saving sensitive information on a shared browser.

Job listings

Job listings are obtained from third-party providers. When you follow an application link, you go to the original provider or employer website, which has its own privacy practices and terms.
