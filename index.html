Interview Prep

Tell me about yourself.

Give a 60–90 second summary focused on your recent experience, relevant skills and the role you want.

Why should we hire you?

Connect 2–3 strengths to the employer's requirements and give a short example.

What is your biggest achievement?

Use Situation → Action → Result and include measurable impact when possible.

Why do you want this job?

Show that you understand the role and explain how it matches your skills and goals.
